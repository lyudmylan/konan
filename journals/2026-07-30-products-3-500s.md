# products/3 detail-page 500s — unguarded zero-stock division in get_product

## Summary

Root cause identified. The product-detail handler `get_product` (faultline
`shop.py:64`) computes `turnover_score` as `units_ordered / units_in_stock` with no
zero-denominator guard; product 3, "Unobtainium Ingot", is the catalog's only
`units_in_stock=0` row (E9), so every load of its detail page raises an unhandled
`ZeroDivisionError` and returns 500 — 7 of 7 requests in the window failed, 4 of them
real user traffic between 18:25:52Z and 18:25:58Z (E3, E4, E11). All other products
and routes serve correctly, including the same computation for in-stock products
(E10); process health, chaos state, and dependency shapes were checked and ruled out
(H2, H3 invalidated). Confidence is high: the divisor was observed directly in the
data, the raise site is named in the stack, and the failure reproduces on demand
(E8). Impact is confined to product 3's detail page while its stock is 0 — but any
product whose stock reaches 0 joins the same failure mode, which makes the fix
urgent despite the narrow blast radius.

## Investigation

Baseline (orchestrator): health green while user 500s were seconds old (E13) — the
healthy-monitoring trap in its textbook form; metrics already showed the 500s
confined to one route (E6). Process uptime of 27s revealed a restart at the window
head and no earlier telemetry (E1) — recorded as a gap, not silently absorbed.

Triage ran three lenses in parallel, blind to each other — software-defect,
infra-process, interfaces; the complaint's framing could not exclude any of them.
All three converged on the same anomaly from different directions: a deterministic
per-input failure (software-defect), on a stable, unsaturated process
(infra-process), with no injection, dependency, or gateway shape (interfaces).
Findings deduplicated into three hypotheses: H1 (data-dependent code defect), H2
(process instability), H3 (injected/dependency/gateway failure).

Selection: H2 and H3 skipped deep-dive — their triage evidence was already decisive,
and deep-dives fill evidence gaps, not re-prove verdicts. H1 had exactly one open
gap: `units_in_stock=0` was inferred from the stack trace, not observed. One
deep-dive agent probed what the hypothesis predicts: the catalog row itself (E9 —
divisor observed: 0), determinism (E8 — still 500), and the mechanism on in-stock
controls (E10 — same computation succeeds, including a numerator-zero case, 0/75 →
0.0, isolating the denominator as the fatal side). Three disconfirmation directions
were attempted; all came back null.

The judge (fresh context, cited evidence only) validated H1 and invalidated H2 and
H3, with two refinements adopted here: the determinism claim is conditioned on stock
remaining 0 (stock is mutable state — orders exist in the window, E6), and product
4's `price: null` (E10) is recorded as a latent finding rather than ignored.

Budget: one triage round (3 lenses), one deep-dive of a permitted three, one judge —
concluded in round 1. Tokens: subagents alone consumed ~206k; the orchestrator's

## Hypotheses

### H1 — data-dependent code defect (validated)

`GET /products/3` fails deterministically: unhandled `ZeroDivisionError` at
`shop.py:64` in `get_product`, where `turnover_score` divides `units_ordered` by
`product["units_in_stock"]`. The chain is closed at every link: the raise site is
named in the stack of a user-traffic failure (E2) tied by request id to its 500
access line (E3); the divisor is observed as 0 in the catalog data (E9); exception
count equals 5xx count at both measurement points — 4==4 at baseline, 7==7 at close
(E7, E11) — so this exception explains every 500 in the window and nothing else
errors; the failure reproduces on demand (E8) while the identical computation
succeeds for every in-stock product (E10, E5). Four of the seven failures are
unstamped user traffic (E3, E4) — active user impact, not a latent
investigator-triggered finding.

### H2 — process instability (invalidated)

One startup event, uptime monotonic across three snapshots back-computing to the
same ~18:25:42Z start (E1, E13, E14), peak RSS byte-identical between snapshots
(E15), a 200 served in 1.5 ms between two failures (E5), and failure durations of
5–12 ms — nothing timeout- or saturation-shaped, and a route-selective,
request-scoped arithmetic failure leaves instability nothing to explain.

### H3 — injected degradation / dependency / gateway (invalidated)

Chaos gauge 0 at every snapshot spanning incident and reproduction (E12); the only
5xx series in every metrics dump is the one route's 500 — no 502/503/504 exists at
all (E6, E11); the stack terminates in in-process application code doing local
arithmetic, not a dependency call (E2); and the error body is the app's own envelope
echoing the probe's request id (E8) — no intermediary produced these errors.

## Safe next steps

Read-only suggestions; remediation is out of scope for this report.

- Hand to the service owners: guard the `turnover_score` computation in
  `get_product` (`shop.py:64`) against `units_in_stock == 0`. Until then, any
  product whose stock reaches 0 will 500 identically — the blast radius is one
  product today, not one product by design.
- Watch `http_exceptions_total{type="ZeroDivisionError"}` (E7, E11): growth beyond
  investigator probes means ongoing user impact.
- Ask the data owners whether product 3's `units_in_stock=0` is intended
  out-of-stock state or bad data — this surface cannot tell (gap 3).
- Triage the latent finding separately: product 4 serves `price: null` with HTTP
  200 (E10).
